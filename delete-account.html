Privacy PolicyDelete your account or data

Last updated: 17 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your CakesUG account and the personal data connected to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send the request from the Google account you use to sign in to CakesUG, or tell us that email address, so we can confirm it is you. Use the developer contact email shown on the CakesUG page on Google Play or the App Store, or open an issue titled "Delete my CakesUG account" at github.com/Codemate23Aki/cakesug-privacy/issues (do not post sensitive personal details publicly; we will follow up to verify).
  2. Say whether you want to delete only your own profile (for example a marketer or staff member leaving) or the whole bakery (owner).
  3. We confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days, and tell you when it is done.

Before you ask: leaving a bakery in the app

A marketer or team member can leave a bakery themselves: open My Bakery and choose Leave this bakery. A bakery owner can also release a team member from Team. This ends access straight away.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, profile photo and sign-in link to your Google account.
  • Your team memberships and roles.
  • If you are an owner who asks to delete the bakery: the bakery profile, menu, gallery photos, documents and the team's access.

What may be kept

  • Orders, quotations and receipts are business records of the bakery. When a marketer or staff member is deleted, the bakery keeps its past orders; we remove the personal details of the person from them where it is practical to do so.
  • Information we must keep to meet a legal duty or resolve a dispute, only for as long as needed.
  • Copies in routine backups, which are overwritten within 30 days.
Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you also use DevBite with the same Google account, tell us if you want that account removed too.
