CakesUG ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile app for bakeries and cake makers in Uganda. It helps a bakery manage its cake menu, gallery, customer orders, team and marketers, and produce branded quotations and receipts. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the CakesUG Android and iOS apps (package com.devcraft.bakeryug_native_app on Android).

1. Information we collect

WhatDetails

Bakery information you addBakery name, logo, tagline, address, phone, email, brand colours, cake menu (names, descriptions, prices, categories, flavours), and gallery photos.

The app does not use advertising, does not sell personal information, does not include third-party advertising or analytics trackers, and does not ask for your device's precise location or contacts.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your account and your bakery's data secure.
  • To run the features you use: menu, gallery, orders, team, marketers and commission tracking, quotations and receipts.
  • To show a bakery owner the orders, totals and commission that belong to their bakery.
  • To fix problems, prevent abuse and improve the app.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your bakery. Information belongs to the bakery that entered it. Owners and admins can see their bakery's orders, team and marketers. A marketer sees only their own orders and their own commission. Managers and staff see what their role allows.
  • DevBite. If a bakery owner chooses to send an order to DevBite, the order details needed to bake and deliver it (customer name and phone, delivery place, cake details and price) are shared with DevBite, the cake-ordering platform that CakesUG connects to. Nothing is sent until the owner sends it.
  • Service providers. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database and Cloud Storage for photos) and Vercel (hosting for our server). They process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the service.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Where information is stored and security

Information is stored with Google Firebase and Vercel, whose servers may be located outside Uganda. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access is restricted by sign-in and by role. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account secure.

5. How long we keep information

We keep information while your account and your bakery are active. Order, quotation and receipt records are business records of the bakery, so when a team member or marketer is released from a bakery their past orders stay with the bakery. When you ask us to delete your account we remove your personal profile and sign-in data within 30 days, except where we must keep information to meet a legal duty or to protect the bakery's own financial records, in which case it is kept only as long as needed.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. You can view and edit your bakery information, menu, team and documents in the app.
  • Deletion. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time. See how to delete your account or data.
  • Leaving a bakery. A marketer can leave their bakery from the app (My Bakery, Leave this bakery) and an owner can release a team member.
  • Camera and photos. You decide which photos to share; you can withdraw the permission in your device settings.

If you live where data-protection law gives you more rights (for example under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019), you may also ask us to restrict or object to processing, or to give you a copy of your data. Contact us using the details below.

7. Children

CakesUG is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated, and significant changes will be announced in the app or on our Google Play listing.

9. Contact us

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: use the developer contact email shown on the CakesUG page on Google Play or the App Store, or open an issue at github.com/Codemate23Aki/cakesug-privacy/issues (please do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue).
